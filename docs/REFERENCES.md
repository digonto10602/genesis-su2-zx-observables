# References and implementation sources

## SU(2) lattice gauge theory

- J. Kogut and L. Susskind, “Hamiltonian formulation of Wilson's lattice gauge theories,” *Physical Review D* 11, 395 (1975), <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.11.395>.
- S. A. Rahman, R. Lewis, E. Mendicelli, and S. Powell, “Self-mitigating Trotter circuits for SU(2) lattice gauge theory on a quantum computer,” *Physical Review D* 106, 074502 (2022), <https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.09247>.
- L. Ebner et al., “Entanglement entropy of (2+1)-dimensional SU(2) lattice gauge theory on plaquette chains,” *Physical Review D* 110, 014505 (2024), <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.110.014505>.
- I. Raychowdhury and J. R. Stryker, “Loop, string, and hadron dynamics in SU(2) Hamiltonian lattice gauge theories,” *Physical Review D* 101, 114502 (2020), <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.101.114502>.

## ZX calculus

- J. van de Wetering, “ZX-calculus for the working quantum computer scientist,” <https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.13966>.
- R. Duncan, A. Kissinger, S. Perdrix, and J. van de Wetering, “Graph-theoretic simplification of quantum circuits with the ZX-calculus,” *Quantum* 4, 279 (2020), <https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2020-06-04-279/>.
- PyZX documentation, <https://pyzx.readthedocs.io/en/latest/>.
