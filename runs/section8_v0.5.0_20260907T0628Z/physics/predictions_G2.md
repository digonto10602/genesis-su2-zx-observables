# Predictions for G2 (written before exact-dynamics results exist)

Derived by the physics-review lane (delegated child summary, transcribed by
the scribe; the dynamics builder had produced no numbers at write time).

1. Resonance: string breaking fastest near m* ≈ 3g²/16 (tree level; at g2=1,
   m* ≈ 0.1875). Finite-size/coupling corrections from hopping-induced level
   repulsion and magnetic mixing shift m* at O(10–50%); direction determined
   numerically. P_surv should drop substantially on t ~ few/(hopping element
   ~ 1/2), i.e. t_b ~ 2–10 lattice units.
2. Channels: both open near resonance; meson channel dominant, baryon–
   antibaryon subdominant (second-order pair-creation process), following the
   2+1D SU(2)-with-matter phenomenology of Cataldi et al. (2311.15926).
3. Off resonance (m << m* or m >> m*): P_surv oscillates with small leakage;
   coherent stretched <-> short shortening (magnetic + hopping) dominates.
4. Casimir: at breaking, Σ_l⟨E²_l⟩ drops from 9/4 toward ~3/2 (meson configs
   (0,½,0,½)/(½,0,½,0) have 2 excited links): ΔC ≈ -3/4 at strong breaking.
5. Pair-creation onset: ⟨n_3⟩ and ⟨2-n_4⟩ rise from 0 in step with
   P_meson + P_BBbar.
6. Conservation: ⟨H⟩ and N exact (≤1e-10 numerically).
7. Truncation: jmax=1 corrections < 0.1 on probabilities for g² ≥ 1
   (magnetic coupling 1/(2g²) suppresses higher flux); at g² = 0.5 may exceed.

Sign-off checklist for G2: resonance within ~50% of 3g²/16; meson weight >
BBbar weight near m*; Casimir reduction present; expm-vs-Krylov ≤ 1e-9;
energy/N drift ≤ 1e-10; window criteria per prompt §6 Phase 2.
